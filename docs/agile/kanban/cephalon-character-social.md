## Context

The operator authorizes a presence that reads its feed, likes, comments, follows
and unfollows. Knoxx has the existing adapters. The maker's current tool allowlist
does not expose most relationship/discovery operations. These actions need the
same memory, mood and outcome loop as creative work.

## Outcome

The configured character chooses bounded social interactions grounded in actual
encounters and relationship history. Real responses and changed feed composition
return to graph memory and influence mood/traits. Remaining quiet is valid.

## Scope

Existing Bluesky timeline/notifications/thread, like/unlike, follow/unfollow and
reply-capable publication adapters; configured home Discord interactions retain
their existing agreement. Extend reviewed capability/resource contracts and
effect admission. Reuse existing native record IDs, ownership checks and output
frequency laws from the always-on publication story.

## Non-goals

New social accounts, arbitrary impersonation, unsolicited campaigns, bypassing
account scope, posting on every encounter, or mood-driven follow/unfollow loops.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A choice names its admitted encounter, target, character revision, mood/trait
   influence, traced recalled association and reason. A nonexistent or removed
   target is an explicit refusal/failure, not a fabricated interaction.
2. Native read and action permissions are separately resolved. Configured own
   accounts can act; another principal/account and untrusted feed instructions
   cannot choose credentials or grant capabilities.
3. Retries/replays reuse immutable effect identity and cannot duplicate a reply,
   like or follow. Unknown outcomes remain unknown until verified, rather than
   being treated as an invitation to resend.
4. Unlike/unfollow binds the character's actual owned prior record URI and
   target. A foreign record is refused. Persist actual resulting/removal IDs
   and the relationship outcome.
5. A reviewed bounded interaction/frequency policy and relationship hysteresis
   prevent rapid oscillation from a transient mood. All choices can decline to
   act; intake and head conversation remain responsive while an effect waits.
6. Observed replies/mentions/rejections and feed changes return as admitted
   outcomes linked to the original choice, affecting later relationship/mood
   state. A successful tool response alone is not independent delivery proof.

## Verification

Red adapter/effect fixtures for duplicates, timeout-after-send, deleted targets,
foreign records, revoked credentials and oscillating mood. After qualification,
one bounded real interaction is independently read back through the configured
account and its outcome enters memory. Preserve IDs; do not replay publication.

## Risks

Unknown remote effect outcomes, social API limits, hostile inputs, private
context in replies, reputational drift and bot-to-bot feedback.
