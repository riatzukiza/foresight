## Context

The character spans maker, conversational and social facets. Role prompts,
credential-bearing actors and sticky transcript windows currently supply parts
of continuity, but they are distinct from a durable presence and trait model.
Repeated motifs in a role should remain an available creative lens.

## Outcome

One stable character presence has a durable creative role, evidence-supported
interests, tastes, habits, commitments and relationship history. Slow traits
develop across episodes, while fast mood and temporary attention remain separate.

## Scope

Existing identity bindings plus a reconstructible character-state projection in
Knoxx, with graph links to supporting encounters/outcomes. Shared snapshots feed
facets under visibility/budget rules. Separate durable role/identity from optional
lore and repeated production recipes; retain an explicit operator-edit path.

## Non-goals

Infer personhood, overwrite platform credentials or canonical actor identities,
merge characters by name/embedding similarity, assign lasting traits from one
response, or preserve every transcript message in prompt context forever.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Presence, credential principal, platform account, session, role, mood and
   trait projection have distinct identities and explicit authorized bindings.
   A renamed account/session cannot create or merge presence silently.
2. Trait updates name supporting episodes/outcomes, confidence, timescale and
   revision. Repeated supported choices can evolve a trait; one fluctuation in
   mood or one like does not rewrite it. Contradictions remain inspectable.
3. Head, maker and social facets read the same admitted character revision,
   with visibility-safe snapshots. Cross-facet concurrent updates and replay
   cannot lose commitments or fabricate evidence.
4. Restart and context pruning preserve lasting identity, preferences,
   relationships and unfinished work through the graph/projection, separately
   from the bounded temporary transcript.
5. A chosen interest/relationship changes foraging or creative selection with
   linked evidence; the character can choose a new subject/medium/structure
   without compulsory repeated lore motifs or fallback recipes.
6. Operator corrections are attributed events with explicit authority and
   effects. They do not rewrite original encounter or trait-history receipts.

## Verification

Red identity/binding, slow-change, contradiction, cross-facet conflict and
restart/pruning cases. Paired scenarios demonstrate an evidence-supported
preference affecting a choice; inspect the actual selected memories and state.

## Risks

Credential identity mistaken for character identity, false personality claims,
unsupported trait inference, commitment loss and self-reinforcing taste collapse.
