## Context and outcome

one outside encounter changes persistent graph and real physical field; one
separate deterministic mood transition is persisted and consumed; the automatic
maker recalls an eligible encounter under its actual stored principal and the
attributable graph trace reaches its actual provider prompt. The accepted
`2026-10-08-cephalon-loop-slice-milestone.md` remains the finish condition.
No new character behavior is added. No subset completes the larger story.

## Proposed dependency handoff

Encounter admission + qualified scoped graph interface + completed selection
handoff feed finite physical delivery. ABI/time precedes force coupling, which
precedes contact/inclusion/replay. Their completed physical contract plus the
encounter and graph interface feed atomic persistence. Completed persisted
finite field feeds the separate mood transition/consumer. Graph reads complete
before the separately dependent feedback writer, so ABI/atomic work need not
wait for that writer. Qualified deployment consumes graph, atomic, mood and
feedback; served causal proof follows deployment, and terminal closeout follows
that proof. The original automatic card is their integration parent.

The encounter packet completes only the admitted envelope and prepared effect
identity, not a graph projection or a successful B1 write. Physical computation
uses the prior authorized stored snapshot and a validated candidate projection
of that envelope. Both remain immutable inputs; the candidate is not written
or exposed as committed state. Its authorization comes from current stored
authority and actual admission, never from the payload or a guessed grant.
The atomic packet commits the planned graph projection, physical field and
effect record together against expected revisions, then independently reads
the pair back. That completed packet supplies the selected intake graph-write
proof and B1 persistence proof. Failed or uncertain commit leaves the projection
pending/refused or quarantined. No early graph write and no dependency on a
later successful commit are hidden inside the admission packet.

The prepared effect identity is bound to the canonical external encounter and
content revision, not an independently generated identifier per attempt.
Admission retains edits and predecessor attribution and deduplicates the same
external revision before preparing physical work. Its selection reason and
actual seed remain inspectable; a changed hash cannot silently become an
unrelated encounter.

The separate feedback packet owns the deliberate feedback contract and its
storage implementation/tests after the read interface completes. Read-only traversal does not implicitly
reinforce. Feedback requires a verified observed downstream outcome attributed
to the recalled item, current stored authority and a stable recall/outcome
effect identity. Inclusion evidence identifies the item; inclusion alone or
retrieval count cannot authorize reinforcement. Missing/unverified outcome
refuses without reinforcement, including an inclusion-with-no-outcome fixture.
Repeated recall/submission/replay cannot multiply feedback.
Partial, failed or uncertain writes remain explicit and require actual recovery
readback or quarantine before retry. Source qualification uses bounded owning
storage fixtures and does not require the later live maker proof to complete;
the automatic-proof packet must additionally observe this once-only behavior
on the selected actual loop. Existing graph-only source provides no feedback
implementation merely by exposing a read API.

The larger recall story retains reserved-head work; the larger field retains
its complete mechanics; the larger mood retains sustained appraisal/continuity.
Their original aggregate completion edges remain meaningful for those later
outcomes. Do not declare an aggregate Done or remove an existing edge merely
because one selected packet passed. The initial child inputs and exact UUID edges are now created through Rheos
for planning review. After qualification, adopt fair aggregation and readiness
through Rheos, requiring native full-graph readiness before RED.
If that lawful adoption is unavailable, retain the owner gap explicitly.
